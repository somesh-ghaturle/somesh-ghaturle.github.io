Reliable, Trustworthy AI

I'm an AI engineer who builds production Generative AI and agentic systems in Python, and I care as much about making them reliable and trustworthy as making them work.

Most of my recent work is enterprise RAG and AI agents. At Pyramid Consulting, I build retrieval pipelines over 15M+ enterprise documents on Databricks Vector Search, covering document ingestion, chunking, embeddings, metadata management, vector and semantic search, re-ranking, grounding, and source citations. That work cut manual review effort by 35%. I design agentic workflows in LangChain and LangGraph with tool use and human approval gates, and I ship them as FastAPI microservices on Docker and Kubernetes with CI/CD, taking deployment cycles from two days to six hours across 20+ services at 99.9% uptime.

Evaluation is where I spend a lot of my attention: hallucination mitigation, faithfulness and retrieval quality checks, guardrails, and observability that catches problems before users do. In my open-source project Agentic-AI-Systems, I built trace-level evaluation that scores an agent's full execution path instead of just its final answer, along with red-teaming tests for prompt injection and MCP tool servers. My research project FAIR-Agent, presented at SARD 2025, improved faithfulness by 17.4% on financial and medical benchmarks.

Before AI, I spent years as a data engineer, and that shapes how I build today. I've worked in regulated environments including healthcare data under HIPAA, migrated 10TB+ of legacy ETL, and built GenAI agents and RAG on AWS SageMaker at BMW Group that cut manual query time by about 40%.

Tools I use most: Python, LangChain, LangGraph, Databricks, MLflow, FastAPI, Docker, Kubernetes, Azure, AWS, and Claude Code.
